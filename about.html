About Pathwise AI

Built by students, for students

AI showed up in our classrooms faster than anyone could explain it. Pathwise AI is our answer: a clear, interactive, honest guide to understanding and using AI well.

Our mission

To demystify artificial intelligence for every high school student, so they can use it confidently, critically, and ethically.

Our vision

A generation that treats AI as a powerful tool they understand, not a magic box they blindly trust or fear.

Our values

Clarity over jargon. Doing over reading. Honesty over shortcuts. Privacy by default.

How we designed Pathwise

Learning science first

Every lesson is short (6–8 minutes) to respect attention spans, ends with key takeaways for review, and is followed by retrieval practice through quizzes and labs. Research shows that retrieving information is one of the most effective ways to remember it.

Gamification with purpose

XP, levels, and badges reward learning behaviors like finishing lessons, mastering labs, and improving quiz scores. Quiz XP is only awarded for beating your personal best, so the system can't be farmed by guessing.

Accessible to everyone

Pathwise aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines: semantic HTML, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, screen-reader labels and live announcements, sufficient color contrast in both light and dark themes, and reduced-motion support.

Private by design

There are no accounts, cookies, ads, or analytics. Progress is stored only in your browser's local storage, which suits a site for students under 18.

Under the hood

HTML5

Semantic, accessible markup across 8 pages.

CSS3

A custom design system with tokens, grid, and theming. No frameworks.

Vanilla JS

Hand-written progress engine, labs, and quizzes. No libraries.

Canvas & SVG

Original animated neural network and generated badge art.
